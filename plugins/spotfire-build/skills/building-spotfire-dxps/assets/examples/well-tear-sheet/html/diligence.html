Diligence flags
15% hurdle not met High
IRR is 10.3%. The maximum bid at a 15% IRR is -$306K, so the $900K price only clears a 10% hurdle (maximum bid $1.00M).
Completion capex drives the outcome High
Completion capex of $9.50M is 10.6 times the purchase price. A 15% overrun moves PV10 by -$1.41M.
Thin margin over the long-term oil deck Medium
The NPV10 breakeven of $62.36/bbl is $0.64/bbl below the $63.00/bbl flat deck. Oil $10/bbl lower moves PV10 by -$1.50M.
Steep first-year decline Medium
Gross rate falls from a 530 boe/d peak to a 173 boe/d year-two average (67% lower).
Long tail carries undiscounted value Low
Years after 2035 hold 36% of remaining net BOE and 74% of undiscounted cash flow, but discounted cash flow after 2035 adds $926K to NPV10.
Location not provided Low
The source case has no location, so offset wells, spacing and basin differentials cannot be checked.
Method
  1. Economics are net to a 100% working interest and a 75% net revenue interest.
  2. Cash flows are pre-tax and unlevered, discounted at 10% mid-month to the Jan 01, 2026 effective date.
  3. Oil starts at $72.00/bbl and declines to $63.00/bbl flat; gas is $3.100/mcf flat. Realized prices vary by year.
  4. Lease operating expense is fixed per well-month, variable per boe, plus gathering and transport per mcf. Production taxes are severance plus ad valorem, both a share of revenue.
  5. Completion capex of $9.50M is spent in Feb-2026 with first production in Mar-2026; $120K of abandonment cost falls at the economic limit (Mar-2076).
  6. Sensitivities rerun the monthly model with an economic limit and apply the change to the base case.
  7. This test build reconstructs the monthly profile and sensitivities from the annual summary (toy data); annual totals match the source exactly.
  8. Source: make_source_economics.py, case DEMO-WELL-001.
