Method

Hydraulic power

Hydraulic (water) horsepower is the power the pump adds to the fluid.

WHP = Q * H * SG / 3960
Efficiency (%) = WHP / shaft power * 100
QFlow rate (US gal/min); 1 bbl/d is 42 / 1440 US gal/min
HHead (ft)
SGSpecific gravity of the fluid (dimensionless)

Assumptions

  • Readings without a positive shaft power are left out of the efficiency.
  • A pump needs the minimum number of readings before it gets a status.
