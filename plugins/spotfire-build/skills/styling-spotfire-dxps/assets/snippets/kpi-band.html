{{control:kpiFleetEfficiency}}
Fleet efficiency (%) iMean efficiency of every valid reading in the period.
{{control:kpiPumpsBelowTarget}}
Pumps below target iPumps whose mean efficiency is below the target in the settings card.
{{control:kpiFlowRate}}
Flow (bbl/d) iTotal flow of the selected pumps on the last day of the period.
{{control:kpiHydraulicPower}}
Hydraulic power (hp) iFlow times head times specific gravity divided by 3960, summed over the selected pumps.
{{control:kpiReadings}}
Valid readings iReadings with a positive shaft power, out of all readings in the period.
{{control:kpiAlarms}}{{control:kpiAlarmsCritical}}
Pumps in alarm iPumps with a reading beyond the critical limit. The value turns red when any pump is in alarm.
